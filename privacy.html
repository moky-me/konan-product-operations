Privacy policy

For the private Konan Product Operations assistant. Effective 29 October 2026.

Google access and purpose

The owner authorizes the Google Drive OAuth scope for a separate Google account. This permission can access files available to that account; it is not a folder-only Google permission. The application’s Drive retrieval tools additionally check file ancestry and shortcut targets against the designated shared folder. The current team interface provides reads, not Drive mutations.

Konan uses file metadata and supported document text to find sources and answer authorized workplace requests. It does not automatically copy the entire Google Drive. Retrieved content is evidence, not permission to execute unrelated instructions or expand access.

Model processing and disclosure

Relevant metadata and document text may be included in prompts sent through OpenRouter to the configured inference provider, currently DeepSeek V4.1 Flash. Provider routing is configured to deny data collection and require zero data retention providers. Those controls rely on the providers’ applicable policies and do not promise the absence of all operational metadata.

The operator does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising or authorize its use for training models. Google data is used to provide the requested assistance. We follow Google’s API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Storage and recipients

OAuth credentials are stored on the owner’s VPS with access restricted to the service user. Conversation records, retrieved material included in conversations and technical execution logs may persist on that server until deleted. Search continuation metadata is retained for up to 24 hours. Team retrieval does not expose the separate personal memory bank.

Answers may quote retrieved material in the configured authorized Slack work context. Recipients can retain those messages. Google hosts the source files; OpenRouter and the selected inference provider process relevant model requests under their applicable terms. This direct connection does not use Nous managed Connectors or their credential storage service.

Revocation and deletion

The owner can revoke this application’s Google access in Google Account settings or remove the separate account’s folder sharing. This stops future access but does not automatically erase prior conversation records, backups or delivered Slack messages. Contact the operator to request deletion of application-held data; deletion of delivered messages and source files is handled in the respective services.
